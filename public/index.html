Questions homeowners ask us

Can you repair just part of my roof, siding, or gutters?

Often, yes. It depends on the extent of the damage, surrounding materials, and whether a localized repair will hold up.

Do you offer full replacements?

Yes, when appropriate and supported by available qualified subcontractors. Our starting point is understanding whether a repair is the better option.

Can I send photos?

Yes. Photos can help us understand the issue, but some problems need an on-site inspection.

Who actually does the work?

NoCo coordinates work with qualified local subcontractors. We clarify the proposed scope, price, and next steps before work is approved.

Do you serve my address?

We generally focus on homes within about an hour of Windsor. Travel times and contractor availability vary, so please share your address for confirmation.

Questions we would ask, too

A few things worth knowing before you hire anyone.

Are you the contractor who actually performs the work?

Under the proposed model, NoCo Home Co. would manage the customer agreement and project while appropriately qualified independent trade contractors perform specialized work. We will explain responsibilities and credentials before any work is authorized.

How do I know the price is fair?

We plan to provide written scope, pricing and relevant options so you can compare what is included. Fair doesn't necessarily mean lowest—it means understanding what you're getting and making an informed choice.

What if I'm not sure which trade I need?

That's okay. Begin with what you're noticing. We'll help identify a sensible next step and whether an in-person assessment is needed.

Does reaching out mean I'm committing to the job?

No. Starting an inquiry does not authorize any work. The live business will publish its actual assessment fees, if any, and contract terms before launch.
