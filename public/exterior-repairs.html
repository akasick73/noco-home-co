A helpful place to start

What might be going on?

It's difficult to identify the full scope of an exterior repair from one visible symptom. Our goal is to help homeowners understand the reasonable possibilities before committing to work.

When it makes sense to coordinate

Gutter damage with wood deterioration; siding affected by water; multiple repairs around doors, windows or eaves.

What we look at first

Which areas are affected, whether there are safety or moisture concerns, and whether different repairs depend on one another.

What you should receive

A clear explanation of proposed work, responsibility for each part of the job, pricing and timing before authorizing repairs.

When to seek prompt professional help: active water entering the structure, unsafe decking, loose overhead materials, or dangerous electrical conditions. Avoid unsafe DIY inspection and use emergency services where appropriate.

What happens after you contact us?

You'll describe the issue and can share photos. We then determine whether an assessment is appropriate and explain how a written estimate would be developed. We won't promise a diagnosis or fixed price without sufficient information.

What is a fair estimate?

One that explains the proposed scope, materials, assumptions, exclusions and payment terms clearly enough to compare. Where multiple repair approaches are reasonable, we want you to understand their tradeoffs.
